webtilp.js and webtilp.wasm are the calculator link library of WebTILP: TILP's libraries (libticonv, libtifiles2,
libticables2, libticalcs2: GPL-2.0-or-later) compiled with Emscripten, with GLib (LGPL-2.1-or-later), libusb
(LGPL-2.1-or-later) and its WebUSB backend, libarchive (BSD-2-Clause), libffi (MIT) and zlib (zlib).

Built by the "Build WebTILP" workflow of https://github.com/adriweb/tilibs (run 44204684450),
from commit e271970086288661692f925960367bbe5118a0e8:
  https://github.com/adriweb/tilibs/tree/e271970086288661692f925960367bbe5118a0e8
The source of every part is listed below (webtilp-deps.txt from the same build). Update them with
public/webtilp/update.sh.
