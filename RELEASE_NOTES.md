## TIde 1.3.1

- **Calculator tab:** the calculator opens in its own tab beside your files, so you can switch between it and your code like any other tab. The calculator keeps its state while you work in another tab. Close the tab, or click the calculator icon again, to put it away.
- **Reload ROM:** a button on the calculator bar that turns the calculator off and boots it again from the ROM, with a fresh memory. It also loads a ROM you just changed with Choose ROM….
